1. Content Security Policy & Zero-CDN Architecture

Athenaeum Ikedi implements an uncompromising self-hosted asset policy. No external scripts, remote web fonts, analytics beacons, or third-party stylesheets are permissible under our active SECURE_CSP configuration.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Privileged routes—including catalog addition, copy withdrawal, student record inspection, fines settlement, and platform parameters—are protected via non-bypassable server-side mixins (LibrarianRequiredMixin). Superuser permissions automatically synchronize with staff privileges.

3. Forensic Audit Trail

Every checkout, renewal, return, fee settlement, book withdrawal, and administrative role change triggers an append-only event recorded in the institutional AuditLog database table with user, timestamp, target entity, and contextual metadata.
