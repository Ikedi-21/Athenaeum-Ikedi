1. Content Security Policy & Self-Hosted Assets

Athenaeum Ikedi serves all assets locally. External scripts, third-party fonts, tracking beacons, and remote stylesheets are restricted under the active SECURE_CSP configuration.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Privileged routes—including catalog management, copy withdrawal, student record access, fee settlement, and system configuration—are guarded by server-side mixins (LibrarianRequiredMixin). Superuser permissions automatically synchronize with staff privileges.

3. System Audit Trail

Circulation actions, catalog updates, fine settlements, copy withdrawals, and administrative role modifications generate an event in the AuditLog table with user, timestamp, target entity, and contextual details.
